# End-User Custody Actions Remain Wallet-Authorized

End users may access LIME through a third-party Market Operator's Frontend and Backend, but custody actions remain wallet-authorized transactions with LIME Programs. Users deposit Collateral, Withdraw Available Collateral, and Claim Payouts or Refunds through signed transactions, while Buy and Sell intent is expressed through off-chain Signed Orders and Trade Execution can be submitted by a Market Operator, relayer, cranker, or other actor. This keeps custody consent explicit without requiring users to submit fill transactions themselves.
