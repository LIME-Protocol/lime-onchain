# Third-Party Market Operator Integration

LIME's on-chain Programs are the protocol surface, while third-party Market Operators may build their own Frontend and Backend to offer prediction-market experiences. Those systems can collect user Signed Orders, operate an Order Book, and submit on-chain transactions, but they are not trusted protocol authorities; the Programs remain responsible for validating Signed Orders, Collateral, Positions, Resolution, Settlement, Claims, and Refunds. This supports LIME as infrastructure rather than a single vertically owned application.
