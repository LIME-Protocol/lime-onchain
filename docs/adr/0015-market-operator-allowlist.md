# Market Operator Allowlist

Market creation is permissioned through a LIME/Admin-controlled Market Operator Allowlist for this cycle. LIME/Admin chooses partner wallets that may create Markets, while end users remain permissionless participants who can sign Orders through a Market Operator's Frontend and Backend. This gives partners operational autonomy without exposing v1 Market creation to spam, weak Resolution Sources, low-quality Markets, or reputational risk before audit and Oracle maturity.
